### Part 1: Install MetaMask
1. Open Chrome, Firefox, Brave or Edge.

2. Go to https://metamask.io/download. Check that the URL is correct, because fake MetaMask sites exist.

3. Click Install MetaMask for Chrome (or your browser). This takes you to the Chrome Web Store.
Click Add to Chrome, then Add extension.

4. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin MetaMask so the fox icon stays visible.

### Part 2: Create your wallet

6. The MetaMask welcome page opens. Tick I agree to the terms of use.

7. Click Create a new wallet.

8. Choose whether to share usage data (No thanks is fine).

9. Create a password. It must be at least 8 characters. It only protects this browser's copy of the wallet.

10. Watch the short video about the Secret Recovery Phrase, then click Secure my wallet.

11. Reveal your 12-word Secret Recovery Phrase.
  
    - Write it on paper in the correct order.
  
    - Never screenshot it, email it or share it. Anyone with it controls your funds.

14. Confirm the phrase by selecting the words you are asked for, then click Confirm.
  
8. Click Done. Your wallet is created.

### Part 3: Explore the wallet
1. Your account address starts with 0x.... Click it to copy it.

2. The default network is Ethereum Mainnet, which uses real money.

3. Click the network dropdown, enable Show test networks, and switch to Sepolia.

4. To rename the account, open the three dots next to it and choose Account details.

5. To add another account, click the account name, then Add account.

### Part 4: Get test crypto (for the transaction demo)
1. Go to a Sepolia faucet, such as https://cloud.google.com/application/web3/faucet/ethereum/sepolia or https://sepolia-faucet.pk910.de.

2. Paste your wallet address and request test ETH.

3. After a minute or two, the balance shows in MetaMask (for example, 0.05 SepoliaETH).

### Part 5: Make a transaction
1. Create a second account in MetaMask, or use a friend's address.

2. Click Send.

3. Choose the recipient (your second account) and enter an amount, such as 0.001 ETH.

4. Click Next, review the gas fee, then click Confirm.

5. Open the Activity tab. The status goes from Pending to Confirmed.

6. Click the transaction and choose View on block explorer (Etherscan) to see the transaction hash.
